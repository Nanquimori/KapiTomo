# Security Policy

## Supported version

Security fixes target the current version published from `main` and `gh-pages`. Older commits, forks, downloaded copies, and third-party plugin repositories are not maintained by KapiTomo.

## Report a vulnerability privately

Do not open a public issue when a report contains an undisclosed vulnerability, personal data, credentials, or instructions that could be used to harm users.

Send the report to `nanquimori@gmail.com`. For a serious problem in a catalog plugin, you may also use the private [Plugin Hub report form](https://nanquimori.github.io/KapiTomo/plugins/?view=report).

Include, when possible:

- the affected URL, plugin ID, file, or commit;
- reproducible steps and the observed impact;
- the smallest safe proof of concept;
- relevant device, browser, or app version details;
- a contact address for follow-up.

KapiTomo will review the evidence before changing a catalog entry. Report quantity is not evidence and never causes automatic hiding or removal.

## Scope

This policy covers the KapiTomo website, its public APIs and catalogs, the Plugin Hub publication automation, and the official KapiTomo plugin. Community plugin code remains in its creator's repository. Report ordinary community-plugin bugs to that creator; report malware, credential theft, impersonation, access-control bypasses, or catalog-integrity risks privately to KapiTomo as well.

## Safe research

Act in good faith, use only accounts and data you are authorized to access, minimize collection, and stop when continued testing could affect another person. Do not perform denial-of-service testing, social engineering, credential attacks, destructive actions, or public disclosure before a reasonable remediation opportunity.

This policy does not authorize activity that violates law, third-party terms, or the rights of other users and services.
